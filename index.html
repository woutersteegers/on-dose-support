On Dose

Medication Reminder & Tracker for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch

On Dose helps you stay on top of your medications with smart reminders, flexible scheduling, and detailed adherence tracking — all while keeping your data private.

Features

  • Flexible scheduling — daily, multiple times per day, every X hours, specific days, cyclical, and more
  • Snooze reminders when you need a few more minutes
  • Adherence tracking with streaks, rates, and calendar history
  • iCloud sync across iPhone and iPad
  • Apple Watch app and complications
  • Home Screen widgets
  • Apple Health integration
  • Refill tracking
  • Export to CSV or PDF
  • Face ID and Touch ID lock
  • Completely free — no subscriptions, no ads

Frequently Asked Questions

Is On Dose really free?

Yes. Every feature is free with no paywalls or subscriptions. An optional tip jar is available if you'd like to support development.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, data is stored in your personal iCloud account. No data is sent to external servers.

Does it work on Apple Watch?

Yes. On Dose includes a watchOS app where you can view upcoming doses and confirm them directly from your wrist.

Can I share my records with my doctor?

Yes. You can export your medication history as a CSV file or a formatted PDF report from the History tab.

Privacy Policy

On Dose does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All medication data is stored locally on your device and, if enabled, in your personal iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit framework.

On Dose does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking. No account or sign-up is required.

If you grant permission, On Dose can read and write medication data from Apple Health. This data is handled entirely by Apple's HealthKit framework and never leaves your device.

In-App Purchases (tips) are processed entirely by Apple. On Dose does not receive or store any payment information.
